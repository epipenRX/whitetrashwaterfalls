The Story

Joe Schuler is a licensed journeyman plumber and a certified pool and jacuzzi expert, credentials he earned the hard way: starting with a shovel on a landscaping crew, then working his way through decks, roofs, and outdoor living spaces before building Schuler Construction LLC into a full-service operation. More than a decade later, he's still on-site running every job himself.

That same precision went into designing the Poolside Paradise Waterfall: the exact measuring and fitting process Joe's used on job sites for over a decade, built into a guided kit that gives your above ground pool a custom-fit arc in an afternoon.
